# Design System: Recursive

This file holds two worlds. The primary system, sections Overview through Do's and Don'ts, is
**Recursive: The Tote Slip**, the customer web app in `app/static/` (`index.html`, `app.css`,
`app.js`). The last section, **Operator console: Kursbuch**, is the owner's private control
room in `data_pipeline/dashboard_server.py`. Its tokens carry a `kursbuch-` prefix in the
frontmatter. Neither world borrows the other's rules. A Kursbuch prohibition such as "no shadows"
does not apply to Recursive, and a Tote device such as the slip shadow or the amber board does not
apply to the console.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Tote Slip"**

Every strategy is a printed slip: numbered, staked and stamped, hanging under a live tote board.
Every screen opens with a tote board, a full-width field of racetrack green (`{colors.board}`)
covered in a faint lamp grid. The money figures on it are amber, set in large condensed numerals.
Below the board is the counter (`{colors.counter}`), a cool grey surface. Strategies sit on it as
thermal-white slips with perforated tear-off edges, a serial number (`No. 00012`), and a
rubber-stamp mark that carries their state. The world rejects both the dark neon trading
terminal and the white brokerage card grid. The page is a betting window, not a terminal.

Density is set for a retail trader who reads money first. Each screen has one dominant field, the
board, so its figures always read first. Slips sit at registered scale in an auto-fill grid and
share one sparkline axis, so you compare them by eye rather than by reading. Barlow and Barlow
Condensed carry every word and number. The condensed cut, uppercase and letterspaced, is the
voice of the board: figures, labels, buttons, stamps and nav. Normal Barlow is the voice of
sentences: slip titles, theses, notes and chat.

The world has three pieces of authored motion. A research slip feeds down out of the board's
slot as the agent prints it. A stamp then lands on it with a slight overshoot. The lamp on a LIVE
plate blinks. Everything else is a short state transition, and `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses
all of it.

**Key Characteristics:**
- A green tote board owns the top band of every screen; amber figures appear only on it.
- Strategies are thermal slips with perforated edges, a serial and a rotated rubber stamp.
- State is a stamped word (PASSED, VOID, PAPER, LIVE), never colour alone.
- Paper money and live money are told apart by a service plate on the board: an outline for PAPER, a solid amber fill with a blinking lamp for LIVE.
- One family: Barlow for sentences, Barlow Condensed for figures and labels. No monospace labels.
- Paper depth: one soft slip shadow on the counter; the board itself stays flat.

## Colors

A racetrack palette: one committed green field with amber figures on it, a cool grey counter,
thermal-white slips, near-black ink in three steps, and stamp inks for state.

### Primary
- **Tote Green** (`{colors.board}`): The board field at the top of every screen, the sign-in
  pitch side, and the mobile tab bar. It is also the text colour on amber (the ticket button and
  the LIVE plate).
- **Board Deep** (`{colors.board-deep}`): The recessed figure cells inside the board, the
  avatar disc, and the slot that slips feed out of.
- **Board Seam** (`{colors.board-seam}`): The 1px seam between board cells, the board's bottom
  edge, and the tab bar's top edge.
- **Board Rule** (`{colors.board-rule}`): Hairlines drawn on the board, such as the avatar ring.
- **Board Label** (`{colors.board-label}`): Labels, sub-figures, notes, idle nav and the PAPER
  plate on the board.
- **Board Text** (`{colors.board-text}`): Board titles and hovered nav.

### Secondary
- **Tote Amber** (`{colors.amber}`): The figures on the board, the wordmark, the current nav item
  and its 3px underline, focus rings on green, the ticket button, the LIVE plate fill, and the 6px
  band across the top of a live slip.
- **Amber Soft** (`{colors.amber-soft}`): The 3px focus halo on inputs and the text selection.

### Tertiary
- **Pass Green** (`{colors.pass}`): PASSED and RUNNING stamps, and gate check marks.
- **Void Red** (`{colors.void}`): VOID, FAILED and PAUSED stamps, and gate cross marks.
- **Live Amber Ink** (`{colors.live}`): A darker amber for marking live money on white stock: the
  LIVE stamp, the on state of the live switch, and the research pulse dot. Tote Amber is too light
  to read on a slip, so this is its on-paper stand-in.
- **Gain / Loss** (`{colors.gain}` / `{colors.loss}`): Signed money figures on slips and tables,
  P&L bars, error text, and the danger and armed buttons.
- **Gain Wash / Loss Wash** (`{colors.gain-wash}` / `{colors.loss-wash}`): Fills behind good and
  bad notices.

### Neutral
- **Counter** (`{colors.counter}`) / **Counter 2** (`{colors.counter-2}`): The page surface under
  the board, and the darker step used in the skeleton shimmer.
- **Thermal Slip** (`{colors.slip}`) / **Slip 2** (`{colors.slip-2}`): Slips, panels, the chat
  column, and the shaded half of a split. Slip 2 is for agent messages, the autopilot bar and the
  raw-spec block.
- **Field White** (`{colors.field}`): Text inputs and choice tiles, one step brighter than the
  slip they sit on.
- **Ink** (`{colors.ink}`), **Ink 2** (`{colors.ink-2}`), **Ink 3** (`{colors.ink-3}`): Text in
  three steps. Ink is for titles, figures, the primary button fill and checked choices. Ink 2 is
  for theses, field labels and serials. Ink 3 is for slip meta, column heads, hints and chart axes.
- **Rule** (`{colors.rule}`) / **Rule 2** (`{colors.rule-2}`): Hairlines: Rule for table and
  list dividers, Rule 2 for input, chip and ghost-button borders and the dashed slip dividers.

### Named Rules

**The Amber-Lives-on-Green Rule.** `{colors.amber}` appears as text only on the green board or
the green tab bar. On the counter and on slips, amber appears only as a solid fill (the ticket
button, the live slip band, the LIVE plate). A live mark printed on white stock uses
`{colors.live}`.

**The One Green Field Rule.** Each screen gets exactly one band of `{colors.board}`, at the top.
The counter is never tinted green, and no second board appears further down the page.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Barlow Condensed (falling back to Barlow, then `ui-sans-serif`, `sans-serif`)
**Body Font:** Barlow (with `ui-sans-serif`, `system-ui`, `sans-serif`)
**Label Font:** Barlow Condensed

**Character:** One grotesque family in two widths. The condensed cut, uppercase with 0.08–0.18em
of tracking, sounds like a tote board or a ticket printer. The normal cut is plain and readable
for theses and chat. Barlow Condensed is loaded at 500/600/700 and Barlow at 400/500/600/700.
`body` sets `tabular-nums lining-nums` so every figure lines up in columns.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (Condensed 700, `clamp(44px, 6.4vw, 92px)`, 0.95): The sign-in headline only. Its
  `em` turns amber.
- **Board Figure Lead** (Condensed 600, `clamp(38px, 5vw, 64px)`, 1): The one leading figure on a
  board, for example account value or unseen-data P&L. It is 44px on phones.
- **Board Figure** (Condensed 600, `clamp(26px, 3.1vw, 40px)`, 1): The other board cells. They
  are 28px on phones.
- **Board Title** (Condensed 700, `clamp(28px, 3.4vw, 40px)`, 1.05): The page title on a
  sub-page board, followed on the same baseline by a Board Label deck capped at 62ch.
- **Sheet Title** (Barlow 600, `clamp(26px, 2.8vw, 34px)`, 1.15): The strategy name on its detail
  slip.
- **Section Head** (Condensed 700, 22px, 0.03em, uppercase): Section names on the counter, such as
  ON THE BOARD or AGENTS.
- **Panel Head** (Condensed 700, 17px, 0.08em, uppercase): Panel and chat titles.
- **Slip Title** (Barlow 600, 18px, 1.25): A strategy's plain-language name on a slip.
- **Slip Figure** (Condensed 600, 20px, 1.1): The three figures in a slip's figure row, plus dial
  outputs.
- **Body** (Barlow 400, 15px, 1.5): The page default. A thesis on its sheet is 17px/1.6, capped
  at 68ch. Ledes are capped at 66ch.
- **Label** (Condensed 600, 11–13px, 0.12–0.14em, uppercase): Board cell labels, slip meta,
  serials, column heads, field labels and chat speaker names.
- **Button / Nav** (Condensed 600–700, 15px, 0.1em, uppercase). Small buttons are 13px.
- **Stamp** (Condensed 700, 14px, 0.18em, uppercase). The big stamp on a detail sheet is 22px.

### Named Rules

**The Two-Widths Rule.** Labels, figures and commands are set in Condensed uppercase. Sentences
are set in Barlow in sentence case. A sentence is never uppercased, and a figure is never set in
the normal width.

**The No-Monospace-Labels Rule.** Tickers, serials, timestamps and chart axes are set in Barlow
or Barlow Condensed, and tabular figures handle the alignment. The one place a fixed-pitch face
appears is the collapsed "Exact spec" block of raw machine JSON, which is a code view rather than
a label.

## Layout

The page has two layers. The board spans the full width; its contents and the `main` counter
below it are centred at `{spacing.maxw}` with a fluid `{spacing.gutter}` gutter. The board holds a
60px masthead (wordmark, five condensed nav items, avatar, sign-out). On sub-pages, a board title
and deck follow. Then comes the `figures` strip: a grid of `--cols` equal cells (five by default)
divided by 1px Board Seam gaps, with the first cell (`.lead`) holding the large figure. A board
foot carries the status line with the service plate flush right. On the Dashboard, the ticket
button sits below the status line. The board foot has a -26px bottom margin, so the ticket hangs
over the board's lower edge onto the counter.

The counter starts with 48px of top padding. Sections are 40px apart. Each section is opened by
a section head, with a note set on the opposite baseline. Slips lay out in
`repeat(auto-fill, minmax(min(100%, 320px), 1fr))` with an 18px gap. That is 300px on the Deploy
board. Two-column screens put the main column beside a fixed rail: Dashboard 380px, Strategy
detail 360px, Deploy chat 420px, with a 28–32px column gap. The research printer puts a 340px
dispatch form beside the feed.

**Breakpoints**
- **≤ 1180px:** Two-column screens collapse to one column. The chat column becomes static at
  70vh. The figure strip drops to three columns, and the lead cell spans the full row.
- **≤ 760px:** The nav is hidden and a fixed five-item tab bar on board green replaces it (64px
  plus the safe-area inset, with body padding reserved for it). The figure strip drops to two
  columns and cells marked `.hide-sm` drop out. Splits, Funds and the research printer go to one
  column. Tables marked `.stack` restack into two-column rows. Deploy gets a Board/Chat segmented
  toggle in place of the side-by-side view. The sign-in page stacks the pitch above the counter.

### Named Rules

**The Board-First Rule.** Every authenticated screen opens with the board, and the board opens
with money or the page's own measured result. The counter starts below it, and nothing comes
before the board.

**The Registered-Scale Rule.** Slips in a grid share one size and one sparkline axis, so heights
and lines can be compared without reading the numbers.

## Elevation & Depth

This is a paper world, so it uses a single soft shadow and almost nothing else. Slips, panels,
run rows, notices and the chat column rest on the counter with the slip-rest shadow (values below and in the sidecar).
That shadow is a 1px contact line plus a soft drop, with no hard offset. Linked slips and run rows
lift 2px and deepen the shadow on hover. The board is flat. It gets its depth from inset cells: a
darker `{colors.board-deep}` fill, an inner top shadow, and 1px seams. The slot that slips feed
from is a 10px recessed bar with a dark inner shadow. Buttons gain a short drop shadow only on
hover. The ticket button and tinted notices never carry a shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Slip rest** (`0 1px 1px rgba(20,30,25,.08), 0 10px 24px -14px rgba(20,30,25,.35)`): Slips,
  panels, run rows, chat, plain notices.
- **Slip lifted** (`0 1px 1px rgba(20,30,25,.08), 0 16px 30px -16px rgba(20,30,25,.45)`): A
  hovered linked slip or run row.
- **Button hover** (`0 6px 16px -8px rgba(0,0,0,.45)`): Standard buttons on hover.
- **Board cell inset** (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(0,0,0,.35)`): Figure cells on the board.
- **Slot** (`inset 0 3px 4px rgba(0,0,0,.6)`): The printer slot slips feed out of.

### Named Rules

**The Paper-on-a-Counter Rule.** Things on the counter rest on it with the soft slip shadow.
Things on the board are recessed into it. Nothing on the board floats.

## Shapes

Slips are hard rectangles (`{rounded.none}`) with perforated top and bottom edges. The
perforations are cut by a CSS mask of 3.5px half-circles on a 12px pitch. Interactive controls and
panels take a small 3px corner (`{rounded.slip}`). The service plate is 2px (`{rounded.plate}`).
Chips and switches are full pills (`{rounded.pill}`). Chat bubbles are 10px (`{rounded.bubble}`),
with the corner nearest the speaker tightened to 3px. The ticket button has semicircular 6px
notches masked out of its left and right edges, like a torn ticket stub. Stamps are 2px-bordered
3px-radius boxes rotated -7deg, with a speckle mask that knocks small flecks out of the ink. Dashed
1px rules divide content inside a slip (figure row, foot, list rows). Solid rules are kept for
table heads and splits.

Icons are inline SVG at 24×24, stroke 1.8, round caps and joins, using `currentColor`. The
wordmark glyph is a single-stroke R with a tail, at stroke 6.

### Named Rules

**The Perforation Rule.** Only a slip, meaning a strategy, hypothesis or deployment, gets
perforated edges. Panels and rows are ordinary stock with a 3px corner. If an object is not a
strategy, do not tear it.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** open every authenticated screen with the green board and put money or the page's
  measured result in its lead cell.
- **Do** keep amber text on green only. On white stock, mark live money with `{colors.live}`, the
  amber top band, or a filled amber plate.
- **Do** carry every strategy state as a stamped word. Pair colour with PASSED, VOID, PAPER or
  LIVE so the state survives in greyscale.
- **Do** show the service plate (PAPER outline or LIVE amber fill) on any board that displays
  money.
- **Do** set figures, labels and commands in Barlow Condensed uppercase, and sentences in Barlow
  sentence case. Keep `tabular-nums lining-nums` on.
- **Do** give every new strategy-like object the slip anatomy: serial, title, plain thesis,
  dashed figure row, stamp.
- **Do** use the slip-rest shadow for things that sit on the counter, and recess anything placed
  on the board.
- **Do** keep tap targets at least 44px tall, and keep input text at 16px.
- **Do** honour `prefers-reduced-motion`; the global block collapses every animation and
  transition.

### Don't:
- **Don't** build the dark neon trading terminal or the white brokerage card grid. The counter is
  cool grey, slips are thermal white, and the only dark field is the green board.
- **Don't** use a monospace face for labels, tickers, serials or axes.
- **Don't** perforate panels, rows or forms. Tear edges belong to slips.
- **Don't** add a second board band, a second ticket button, or amber text on the counter.
- **Don't** let paper and live converge. LIVE is always the filled amber plate, stamp or band.
  PAPER is always the outline or grey stamp.
- **Don't** add a third type family or an icon font. Icons are inline 1.8-stroke SVG in
  `currentColor`.
- **Don't** add a build step or framework. The surface is vanilla HTML, CSS and JS served from
  `app/static/`.

## Operator console (data_pipeline/dashboard_server.py): Kursbuch

Scope: the owner's private control room served by `data_pipeline/dashboard_server.py` (CSS and JS embedded in Python string literals). This world was recorded before Recursive existed and is still live. Its tokens appear in the frontmatter with a `kursbuch-` prefix, and its rules (no shadows, no gradients, one reserved signal red, Archivo/Archivo Narrow) apply only to that surface. The sub-sections below keep the original record, with headings demoted one level.

**Original name:** Kalshi BTC 15M Control Room. A Swiss station timetable for a single operator watching real money: ink on stock, hairline rules, one reserved signal colour.

### Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Station Timetable"**

This is a departures board for money, not a trading terminal. Everything is printed on a warm
grey stock, ruled with hairlines, and read top to bottom in a single column of sections. Figures
sit on a rule; they never sit in a card. The page has no shadow, no gradient, no glass, no
blur, and no rounded panel: the only radius in the system is a 3px hairline nick on small
inset objects. Depth is a printing problem, solved by rule weight and by two stock tints,
never by lifting anything off the page.

The world deliberately refuses the terminal look that trading software defaults to. **There is
no monospace anywhere in the build** — not in tables, not in chart axes, not in identifiers.
Alignment of figures is bought with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums` set on
`body` and reasserted on every numeric cell, which is what a timetable actually does. Typography
is one family in two cuts (Archivo and Archivo Narrow) at two weights (400 and 600). The narrow
cut carries every label, head, chip, tab and status line; the normal cut carries sentences and
figures.

The page is built to be read by one operator at a glance, so the system spends its scarcity
budget on a single reserved red. Money direction is never told in that red; it rides a
desaturated tint field behind the figure instead. The whole surface is served as plain CSS and
vanilla JS embedded in Python string literals — there is no build step, no framework, no icon
package, and no runtime dependency beyond one Google Fonts import.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Ruled rows on stock; no cards, no shadows, no gradients, no glass.
- One reserved signal red, spent only on attention and on the live-money boundary.
- Two stocks — grey newsprint for paper, rose-tinted for real money.
- Archivo + Archivo Narrow, two weights, tabular lining figures everywhere.
- No monospace, anywhere, ever.
- One authored motion: a 180ms wash on a figure whose value just changed.

### Colors

A printed palette: warm grey-green newsprint, near-black ink in three steps, two rule greys,
and one reserved red that appears on perhaps two percent of any screen.

- **Signal Red** (`{colors.kursbuch-signal}`): The reserved colour. It marks the status line when the

- **Signal Wash** (`{colors.kursbuch-signal-wash}`): The red's only fill, behind an armed mark and

- **Ink** (`{colors.kursbuch-ink}`): Body text, figures, section rules (2px), active tab, primary button

#### Named Rules

**The Reserved Signal Rule.** `{colors.kursbuch-signal}` means "this needs you" or "this is real money."

marks, the danger button, the notice box, the caret, the live masthead and live active-tab
underline, and the LIVE cutoff marker on a chart. Adding a sixth use dilutes the other five.

**The Money-Rides-a-Field Rule.** Profit and loss direction is told by a tint field behind the
figure (`{colors.kursbuch-pos}` / `{colors.kursbuch-neg}`) with the figure kept in ink — never by colouring the
figure, and never by the reserved red. A losing number is not an alarm; it is a number.

**The Two Stocks Rule.** Paper trading and pipeline sit on `{colors.kursbuch-stock}`. Real money sits on
`{colors.kursbuch-stock-live}` via `body.live-service`, which also swaps the masthead's bottom rule and
the active tab's underline to `{colors.kursbuch-signal}`. The two surfaces must never be confusable at a
glance.

**The State-Not-By-Colour Rule.** Every state that carries colour also carries a word or a
shape: the stale rule goes dashed and the figures get struck through, the marks read OFF / ON /
ARMED / HALT, the status line swaps a check glyph for a triangle. Remove all colour and the page
still reports correctly.

### Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo (with `ui-sans-serif`, `system-ui`, `sans-serif`)
**Body Font:** Archivo (same stack)
**Label Font:** Archivo Narrow (falling back to Archivo, then `sans-serif`)

**Character:** One grotesque family in two cuts and two weights (400, 600). Archivo Narrow is
the timetable voice — condensed, letterspaced, uppercase, used for anything that labels rather
than states. Archivo normal is the reading voice: sentences, figures, table data. There is no
third family and, emphatically, no monospace cut; `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums
lining-nums` is declared on `body` and restated on numeric cells and inputs, which gives column

#### Hierarchy
- **Page Title** (`h1.page`, 600, `clamp(23px,3vw,30px)`, `-0.02em`): One per page, sentence case.
- **Status Line** (`.said`, Narrow 600, `clamp(16px,2.1vw,20px)`, 1.35): The single dominant

- **Figure** (`.fig`, 600, `clamp(21px,2.6vw,27px)`, `-0.015em`, 1.1): The measured numbers in a
  `.measure` row. `.fig.sm` is the 17px variant for denser rows.
- **Section Head** (`.sect-head h2`, Narrow 600, 13px, `0.13em`, uppercase): Names a section

- **Body** (Archivo 400, 14px, 1.45): Page default. Prose is capped at 68ch (`.prose`, `.deck`),

- **Table** (13px; column heads Narrow 600 12px `0.02em`, sentence case): Rows are 9px/12px/8px

- **Micro-label** (`.lab`, Narrow 400, 11px, `0.11em`, uppercase): Above every figure and field.
- **Mark / Button / Chip** (Narrow 600, 11–11.5px, `0.07–0.1em`, uppercase).
- **Wordmark** (Narrow 600, 13px, `0.16em`, uppercase).

#### Named Rules

**The No-Monospace Rule.** No monospace font is permitted anywhere, including chart axis labels
(`11px "Archivo Narrow"`) and code-like identifiers. Numeric alignment comes from tabular lining
figures, not from a fixed-pitch face. The terminal look is the rut this world was built to leave.

**The Short-Caps Rule.** Uppercase is reserved for short labels: section heads, micro-labels,
column-free field labels, chips, tabs, marks, buttons, the wordmark, and the stale mark.
Anything that reads as a sentence — decks, legends, notes, empty-state copy, status-line text,
notice bodies — is sentence case. The rule was tuned against the build: snake_case model names
in tables are left lowercase on purpose, because upper-casing a 29-character identifier costs
legibility and buys nothing.

**The Two-Weights Rule.** 400 and 600 only. There is no 300, 500 or 700 anywhere in the system;
emphasis is bought with the narrow cut, letterspacing, and case instead.

### Layout

A single centred column, `max-width: 2560px`, with a fluid gutter `{spacing.kursbuch-gutter}` and 64px of
foot. Everything stacks vertically: masthead (2px ink rule, red on live) → service nav (1px rule,
three tabs) → page title + deck → status line → sections.

Sections (`.sect`) are separated by `{spacing.kursbuch-section}` of top margin and opened by a `.sect-head`
that flexes its `h2` and `.sect-note` to opposite baselines over a 2px ink rule.

Measured figures use `.measure`: an auto-fit grid of `minmax(150px, 1fr)` cells divided by 1px
vertical rules, closed by a 1px bottom rule. The first cell has no left rule and no left padding,
so the grid aligns flush with the page's text edge.

Tables are fixed-layout (`table-layout: fixed`) with explicit per-table column-width classes
(`.t-books`, `.t-strat`, `.t-period`, `.t-pos8`, `.t-pos7`) and `min-width: 860px`, wrapped in a
horizontally scrollable `.sheet`. Column widths are pinned so a long strategy name cannot swallow
the slack and strand the figures at the far edge of a 2500px sheet. First and last cells lose
their outer padding to keep the flush edge. Column heads are sticky to the top of the viewport
and repaint the current stock behind themselves.

**Breakpoints**
- **≤ 640px** — `.stack` tables restack: `thead` is visually hidden, each `tr` becomes a block
  with a `rule-2` bottom border, and each `td` becomes a labelled row printing `attr(data-lab)`
  in 11px uppercase Narrow on the left with the value right-aligned. Row hover is disabled.
  The status line drops to 15px.
- **≤ 860px** — `.measure` collapses to two columns with the vertical rule only on even cells;
  `.sheet` bleeds to the full device width by negative gutter margins; column heads unstick.
- **≥ 1500px** — `.plates` becomes a two-column grid (12px gap) with the first plate spanning
  full width.
- **≥ 2200px** — `.plates` becomes three columns and the first plate rejoins the flow.
- **Print** — white background; service nav and buttons are removed.

#### Named Rules

**The One Dominant Answer Rule.** Each page opens with exactly one status line stating what the
system is doing right now; every section below is supporting evidence. On a phone that line owns
the first viewport.

**The Flush-Edge Rule.** The leftmost column of any measure grid or table drops its padding and
its rule so figures align to the same vertical as the page title. Nothing is indented into a box.

### Elevation & Depth

**There are no shadows in this system.** Not one `box-shadow`, gradient, blur, or backdrop-filter
exists in the build, and none may be added. Depth is printed: it is carried entirely by rule
weight and by four stock tints.

The rule hierarchy is the elevation scale:
- **2px solid ink** — masthead bottom and section head. The strongest division on the page.
- **2px solid signal** — the same rule on the live masthead; **2px dashed signal** when a section
  is stale.
- **1px `rule-2`** — under a column head, and around inputs and chips.
- **1px `rule`** — row dividers, measure cell dividers, plate borders, service nav bottom.

Tonal layering does the rest: `stock-raised` lifts a chart plate, an input, or a hovered row one
step off the page; `stock-sunk` recesses a scrollbar track. On the live surface the plate
inverts — `body.live-service .plate` uses `{colors.kursbuch-stock}` — so the plate still separates from a
rose page.

#### Named Rules

**The Printed-Depth Rule.** If something needs to separate, rule it or tint it. Never lift it.
A shadow, gradient, or glass panel anywhere in this world is a defect.

### Shapes

Rectangles, everywhere, at zero radius. The only radius token is `{rounded.kursbuch-hairline}` (3px), and
it appears only on small inset objects: marks, chips, buttons, inputs, selects, and the tint
field behind a money figure. Sections, plates, notices, tables, and the page shell are hard
rectangles.

Borders are hairlines (1px) except the two structural 2px rules. The one non-rectangular form in
the system is the pair of authored 24×24 status glyphs — a circled check and a warning triangle —
drawn inline as SVG at `stroke-width: 1.6`, `stroke-linecap: round`, with `stroke: currentColor`
so they inherit ink or signal from the status line. They are authored rather than pulled from a
glyph set, which is what keeps one stroke weight across the page.

#### Named Rules

**The Hairline-Nick Rule.** 3px radius on inset objects, 0 everywhere else. There is no medium
or large radius step, and adding one would break the printed register.

**The Drawn-Icon Rule.** Icons are inline SVG strokes at 1.6 drawn to match the type's weight,
using `currentColor`. No icon font, no glyph package, no emoji.

### Components

#### Buttons
- **Shape:** Hard rectangle with a 3px nick (`{rounded.kursbuch-hairline}`); 1px ink border, transparent fill.
- **Primary:** Narrow 600, 11.5px, `0.1em`, uppercase, padding `6px 13px 5px`.
- **Hover:** Fills ink, text knocks out to `{colors.kursbuch-stock-raised}`, over 160ms
  `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)` on background and colour only.
- **Active:** `translateY(1px)`. Nothing else moves.
- **Disabled:** Border and text drop to `{colors.kursbuch-rule-2}` / `{colors.kursbuch-ink-3}`, cursor `not-allowed`.
- **Danger:** Signal border and text; fills signal with `{colors.kursbuch-signal-ink}` on hover. Used for
  arming or halting live trading only.
- **Small:** 11px, padding `4px 10px 3px`.

#### Chips
- **Style:** Narrow 600, 11.5px, `0.07em`, uppercase; `{colors.kursbuch-ink-2}` on transparent with a 1px
  `{colors.kursbuch-rule-2}` border and a 3px nick. Laid out as an unstyled `ul.chips` with 7px gaps.
- **Hover:** Border and text go to ink. There is no selected state; chips are navigation.

#### Marks (status pills)
The state vocabulary. Narrow 600, 11px, `0.09em`, uppercase, `2px 7px 1px`, 3px nick.
- **Default:** `{colors.kursbuch-ink-2}` on transparent, `{colors.kursbuch-rule-2}` border — e.g. OFF.
- **`.on`:** Solid ink with knocked-out text — the affirmative, permanent state.
- **`.armed`:** Signal text on `{colors.kursbuch-signal-wash}` with a signal border.
- **`.halt`:** Solid signal with `{colors.kursbuch-signal-ink}` text — the loudest object in the system.
- **`.quiet`:** `{colors.kursbuch-ink-3}`, for a state that is true but uninteresting.

#### Containers
There are no cards. The two container forms are:
- **Section (`.sect`)** — an unfilled region opened by a ruled head. No border, no background.
- **Plate (`.plate`)** — the only filled container: 1px `{colors.kursbuch-rule}` border, `{colors.kursbuch-stock-raised}`
  fill (inverted to `{colors.kursbuch-stock}` on the live surface), padding `10px 12px 6px`, zero radius.
  Holds a chart canvas and its `.keys` legend (14px-gapped Narrow 12px entries, each with a 14px
  `border-top: 2px` rule swatch in `currentColor`).

#### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** `{colors.kursbuch-stock-raised}` fill, 1px `{colors.kursbuch-rule-2}` border, 3px nick, padding `6px 9px`,
  full width, tabular figures. Wrapped in `.field` (column flex, 3px gap, `min-width: 104px`) under
  a `.lab` micro-label.
- **Hover:** Border to `{colors.kursbuch-ink-3}`.
- **Focus:** Border to ink, background to `{colors.kursbuch-stock}`, native outline removed. The caret is
  `{colors.kursbuch-signal}` — the one place the reserved colour is decorative rather than alarming.
- **Global focus:** Everything else gets `:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid ink; offset 2px }`.
- **Disclosure (`details > summary`):** Narrow 12px `{colors.kursbuch-ink-3}`; the native marker is removed
  and replaced with a `+` / `−` prefix in `{colors.kursbuch-rule-2}`; text goes to ink on hover.

#### Navigation
- **Masthead:** Wordmark (`KALSHI BTC 15M / <service>`) at the left baseline, `.asof` read-clock
  at the right in Narrow 12px uppercase `{colors.kursbuch-ink-3}` with the time itself in ink 600. Bottom
  rule 2px ink, or 2px signal on the live surface, where the service label also turns signal.
- **Service links:** Three uppercase Narrow 12.5px `0.1em` tabs in a 1px-ruled strip. Hover raises
  the stock and darkens the text. `[aria-current="page"]` goes ink with a 2px ink bottom border
  pulled up by `-1px` to overlap the strip rule — signal on the live surface.
- **Mobile:** The strip wraps; no hamburger, no drawer.

#### Status Line (signature)
`.statusline` is the page's one dominant answer: an authored 17px SVG mark plus a Narrow 600
clamp(16–20px) sentence, capped at 104ch, closed by a 1px rule. Segments are joined by a
`{colors.kursbuch-rule-2}` middot separator. In calm state the check glyph shows in `{colors.kursbuch-ink-3}` and
`em` segments read `{colors.kursbuch-ink-3}`. Adding `.needs` swaps to the warning triangle and turns both
the glyph and the `em` segments `{colors.kursbuch-signal}`. `em` is reset to `font-style: normal` — the
alarm is colour plus glyph, not italics.

#### As-of Rule (signature)
The system's defining component: `.sect` + `.sect-head` (h2 + `.sect-note`) over a 2px ink rule.
The note reads `as of <n>s ago` in Narrow 12px `{colors.kursbuch-ink-3}`, recomputed against each writer's
expected cadence (`age_sec > expect_sec × 6` is stale; an unknown writer reads `never written`).

Adding `.stale` to the section breaks the rule in four coordinated ways:
1. The 2px ink head rule becomes **2px dashed signal**.
2. The note turns `{colors.kursbuch-signal}` and goes 600.
3. The note is prefixed by a `.stale-mark` — uppercase, letterspaced `0.09em`, signal, 600 —
   reading `STALE 13D`, followed by a sentence-case clause naming the writer that stopped.
4. Every figure and table cell inside is struck through (`line-through`, 1px, `{colors.kursbuch-rule-2}`)
   and dropped to `{colors.kursbuch-ink-3}`. The numbers remain readable but are visibly disowned.

#### Charts
Canvas drawn at device pixel ratio, axis labels in `11px "Archivo Narrow"` `{colors.kursbuch-ink-3}`,
outer gridlines `{colors.kursbuch-rule-2}`, inner gridlines `#D7DBD0`, zero line 1px ink. Series cycle the
five trace colours paired with five dash patterns. The live cutoff is a dashed signal vertical
labelled `LIVE`. Band charts fill at 7–10% alpha of their own trace colour.

### Do's and Don'ts

#### Do:
- **Do** separate with a rule or a stock tint. The rule scale is 2px ink / 2px signal / 1px
  `rule-2` / 1px `rule`; the tint scale is `stock-sunk` → `stock` → `stock-raised`.
- **Do** put every new figure block on a `.measure` grid and every new dataset in a `.sect` with
  a `.sect-head` and a live `.sect-note`, so its freshness is always on the page.
- **Do** tell money direction with a `.fig.tint.pos` / `.fig.tint.neg` field behind an ink figure.
- **Do** keep `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums` on anything numeric.
- **Do** reserve uppercase for short labels and write every sentence in sentence case, including
  empty-state copy, notes, and legends.
- **Do** give every new table a `.stack` class and `data-lab` on each `td` so it restacks into
  labelled rows under 640px.
- **Do** pin column widths on any new table via a `colgroup` and a `.t-*` width class.
- **Do** pair colour with a word or a shape for every state.
- **Do** draw icons inline as 1.6-stroke SVG with `currentColor`.
- **Do** honour `prefers-reduced-motion`; the global reduce block collapses all animation and
  transition to 0.001ms.

#### Don't:
- **Don't** add a shadow, gradient, glass/backdrop-filter, or a card. Containers are sections and
  plates only.
- **Don't** use a monospace font anywhere, for any reason, including identifiers and chart axes.
- **Don't** spend `{colors.kursbuch-signal}` on anything but attention or the live-money boundary. Never on
  a losing number, a heading, a link, or decoration.
- **Don't** colour a P&L figure itself; the field behind it carries direction.
- **Don't** introduce a third font family, a third weight, or a radius other than 3px.
- **Don't** uppercase a sentence, a model identifier, or anything longer than a few words.
- **Don't** add a second animation. `.washed` (180ms, `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`, `{colors.kursbuch-rule-2}`
  fading to transparent, fired only when a figure's value actually changed) is the system's one
  authored moment; transitions are limited to the 160ms button colour change.
- **Don't** add a build step, a framework, an icon package, or a runtime dependency. The surface
  is plain CSS and vanilla JS inside Python string literals.
- **Don't** let the live surface and the paper surfaces converge. `body.live-service` must keep
  its own stock, its red head rule, and its red active tab.
