        \begin{itemize}
            \item CFTC-regulated prediction market exchange
            \item Binary contracts: pays \$1 if event occurs, \$0 otherwise
        \end{itemize}
        \vspace{0.15cm}
        \textbf{BTC Up or Down --- 15 Min}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item New contract every 15 min: ``Will BTC be $\geq$ \$X at expiry?''
            \item YES/NO sides trade as probabilities (cents); live bid/ask order book
            \item Settles at \$1 (YES) or \$0 (NO)
        \end{itemize}

\renewcommand{\sectiontitle}{Conclusion}
\section{\sectiontitle}

\begin{frame}{\sectiontitle}
\footnotesize
\textbf{What we did}
\begin{itemize}
    \itemj Built an end-to-end pipeline: Kalshi + Coinbase data, 20s$\to$1-min features, MySQL, shared research and live trading engine
    \itemj Trained six models: naive logistic (baseline), L1 logistic, gradient boosting, random forest, bagging trees, AdaBoost --- with the same train/validation/OOS splits and trading rules where applicable
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.1cm}
\textbf{Results (in-sample vs out-of-sample)}
\begin{itemize}
    \itemj Linear models (baseline, L1): negative OOS PnL; signal near 50/50 --- motivated moving to non-linear models
    \itemj Tree ensembles (GBM, RF): positive OOS PnL, better calibration (AP/AUC $\sim$0.81--0.85); RF more selective (fewer trades, higher win rate)
    \itemj Bagging and AdaBoost: integrated into the research runner; same format and splits; backtest and plots TBD
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.1cm}
\textbf{Takeaways}
\begin{itemize}
    \itemj Feature design (moneyness, order book, BTC flow) and label choice (mid-price vs expiry-resolution) matter as much as model choice
    \itemj Risk and execution discipline (thresholds, position size, drawdown limits) are essential; avoid overfitting to noise
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
