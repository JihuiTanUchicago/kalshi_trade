Observed failures include admitting future-effective rows, calculating incorrect split/dividend returns, treating paid short dividends as unpaid, using historical data limitations to reject the current qualified task, and listing assumptions that do not differ. Timing contributes 10 of memory's 16 hard errors, decisions four and execution two. Context distraction or inappropriate transfer of historical observations is a hypothesis consistent with these responses; this experiment does not isolate a single cause. The same numeric tasks failed under no context as well, especially feature arithmetic.

The frozen memory contains no approved `timing` or `borrow_costs` finding, and the five new records mostly describe past financial comparisons. The ranker can therefore supply broad overlapping facts without a directly applicable worked procedure. Validated source scope alone does not establish usefulness for every task. The two repetitions and 40 templated scenarios are a bounded engineering sample; they do not establish universal provider behavior.

Decision: **revise the mechanism; do not advance the self-improvement claim or paper release**. Retire these inspected cases. A next development stage should use protected deterministic arithmetic/as-of/accounting tools, select smaller actionable procedural context, and separately preregister new tool-using workflow tasks. This direct-answer protocol did not exercise those tools or establish that the full agent workflow improved. Do not teach from these answer keys or change the completed campaign's grader, context or thresholds.
